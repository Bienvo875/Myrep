About us

Who are we?

We are an english club made of young man and woman who gave their lives to Jesus Christ and who are committed to growing spiritually,financially and mentally in order to face all the different challenges of our society in our beautiful country The Democratic Republic of Congo.

Our mission

Our mission is to create a new generation of youngs that first of all know how important It is to serve God and understand all the opportunities the english language can bring into their lives because with that,we are connecting them to the outside world and getting them ready to discover themselves for the future of a country rests on its youth,so having a youth that mentally and spiritually mature,will help our country grow.

Our vision

Our vision is to have a mature and independent youth for a better tomorrow.
